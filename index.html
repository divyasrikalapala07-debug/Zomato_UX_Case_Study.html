UX Case Study: Zomato

A UX case study analyzing the user journey, strengths, weaknesses and possible improvements for the Zomato food delivery platform.

1. Introduction

Zomato is a popular food delivery and restaurant discovery platform. Users can search for restaurants, explore menus, read reviews, compare options and order food online.

This case study analyzes the user experience of Zomato and identifies areas where the experience can be improved.

2. User Journey

1

3. Strengths

Easy Food Discovery

Users can easily search for different cuisines, dishes and restaurants.

Restaurant Information

Restaurant pages provide menus, ratings, reviews and other useful information.

Order Tracking

Users can track the progress of their food order.

4. Weaknesses

Information Overload

A large amount of information and offers can sometimes make the interface feel crowded.

Difficult Comparison

Users may need to open multiple restaurant pages to compare options.

Checkout Complexity

The checkout screen can be simplified by clearly grouping order and payment information.

5. Proposed Improvements

Improvement 1: Smart Restaurant Comparison

Add a comparison feature that allows users to compare restaurants based on price, rating and delivery time.

6. Expected Benefits

  • Faster restaurant comparison
  • Reduced confusion during checkout
  • Better food discovery
  • Improved navigation
  • More convenient ordering experience

7. Conclusion

Zomato provides a convenient platform for discovering restaurants and ordering food. The proposed improvements focus on making the experience simpler, clearer and more user-friendly.

Smart comparison, simplified checkout and personalized search can help users complete their tasks with less effort.
